Why Choose Suresh Suppliers?

To provide sustainable, affordable, high-quality building materials and professional construction solutions for modern Sri Lankan developments.

Quality Manufacturing

Precision-compressed stabilized earth blocks manufactured at our Industrial Zone facility in Nagoda, Kalutara with strict soil screening and curing.

Sustainable Building Solutions

Eco-conscious CSEB bricks produced without kiln firing, conserving timber and reducing carbon emissions while keeping interiors naturally comfortable.

Professional Construction Team

Experienced supervisors, masons, and architectural builders trained in interlocking earth masonry, residential villas, and structural finishes.

Modern Construction Technology

Dual 2.5-inch vertical holes allow concealed steel reinforcement, grouting, and electrical/plumbing conduits with minimal wall chasing.

Reliable Customer Service

Accessible factory support in Nagoda and 4 convenient showrooms in Beruwala, Nagoda, Wadduwa, and Moratuwa for material viewing and consultations.
